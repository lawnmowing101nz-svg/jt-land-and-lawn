Our Services

Seven services, one crew. Regular mowing through the season, plus the bigger jobs most people keep putting off.

Lawn Mowing

Mow, edge and blow down — every visit, not just when it looks bad. Through the Texas growing season we can run weekly or every two weeks depending on how fast your grass moves and how sharp you want it kept.

  • Front, back and side strips mowed at the right height for the season
  • Driveway, sidewalk and bed lines edged
  • Hand trimming around fences, posts, trees and AC units
  • Clippings blown off all the concrete before we leave
Get a mowing price

Hedge Trimming

Hedges and shrubs cut back to an even shape with clean lines — not hacked at. We'll take them back hard if they've gotten away from you, or keep them tight on a regular trim.

  • Hedges, shrubs and foundation plantings
  • Shaped level, front and top, so it reads straight from the street
  • Growth pulled back off windows, walls and walkways
  • All clippings raked up and taken away
Get a hedge price

Overgrown Cleanups

Knee-high grass, weeds through the beds, a rental between tenants, a yard nobody has touched all summer. We bring the right gear for it and get the whole thing back under control — usually in one visit.

  • Tall grass and weeds cut down and cleared
  • Fence lines, sheds and side gates opened back up
  • Vacant lots, rentals and estate properties welcome
  • Green waste hauled off, not left in a pile
Get a cleanup price

Leaf Removal

From October through December the leaves come down faster than most people can keep up with. We clear them off the lawn, out of the beds and off the concrete — as a one-off, or on a run through the fall.

  • Lawns, beds, paths, driveways and patios cleared
  • Leaves bagged and hauled away
  • Gutters and corners the blower can't reach done by hand
  • One-off or repeat visits through the fall
Book a fall cleanup

Junk Removal

The stuff that's been behind the shed for two years. Old furniture, broken fence panels, timber, yard debris, general junk — we load it and it goes. Handy to bundle in with a cleanup while we're already on site.

  • Furniture, appliances, timber and fence panels
  • Yard debris, branches and green waste
  • We do the loading — you don't need to move it to the curb
  • Cheaper bundled with a cleanup on the same visit
Get a haul-away price

Mulch Installation

Fresh mulch is the quickest way to make a yard look finished. We clean the beds out, cut a crisp edge and lay the mulch to depth — which also keeps the weeds down and holds moisture in through the hot months.

  • Beds weeded and cleaned out first
  • Sharp cut edge between bed and lawn
  • Mulch laid to an even depth, kept off the trunks
  • We supply and deliver the mulch
Get a mulch price

Tree Trimming

Low limbs over the driveway, branches scraping the roof, a small tree that's outgrown its spot. We trim back what can be done safely from the ground, and clear the cuttings off site.

  • Low and overhanging limbs cut back
  • Branches cleared off roofs, fences and driveways
  • Small trees shaped or taken out
  • Big removals and anything near power lines we'll refer on
