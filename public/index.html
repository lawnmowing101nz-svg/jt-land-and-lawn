Meet JT Land and Lawn

Run by Joey, Not a Call Center

JT Land and Lawn is a family owned yard service based right here in Saginaw. Joey Trejo started it three years ago and still runs every job himself — so the person who quotes your yard is the person who looks after it.

We're set up for the way Texas lawns actually grow. Mowing runs hard through the summer, cleanups fill the gaps, and once the leaves come down in October we're back with the blowers.
