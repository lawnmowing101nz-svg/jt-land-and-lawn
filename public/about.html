About JT Land and Lawn

A family owned yard service based in Saginaw, looking after lawns across Saginaw and Fort Worth.

Our story

Started by Joey, Still Run by Joey

JT Land and Lawn is a family owned yard service run by Joey Trejo, based in Saginaw. Three years in service, and Joey still quotes and works the jobs himself.

It's a family business, which sounds like a line until you call us. There's no switchboard and no ticket number. You get Joey, he tells you what the job costs, and then he does it.

We're insured on every job, and every job is backed by a straightforward guarantee: if it isn't right, tell us and we'll come back and fix it.

What you get

The Four Things We Promise

Nothing exotic — just the basics done properly, every visit.

3 Years in Service

Long enough to know how St. Augustine and Bermuda behave through a Texas summer, and what a yard needs in December.

Family Owned

The owner is on the job. That means one person is accountable for how your yard looks when we drive away.

Fully Insured

We're insured for the work we do. If you're a landlord or a property manager, we can confirm that in writing.

Satisfaction Guaranteed

If we've missed something or you're not happy with the finish, tell us and we'll come back and put it right.

How a visit works

What Happens When We Turn Up

  • Mow the front, back and side strips at the right height for the season
