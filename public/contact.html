Common questions

Before You Call

A few things people usually want to know first.

Do I have to sign up for a schedule?

No. Plenty of our work is one-off — a cleanup, a hedge, a haul-away. If you want regular mowing we'll set that up, but it's your call.

How often should the grass be cut?

Through the Texas summer, most lawns want it weekly. Some hold up fine on every two weeks. We'll tell you honestly which yours is.

Do you do rentals and vacant properties?

Yes — rentals, vacant lots and properties between tenants. We're insured, and we can bill a landlord or property manager directly.

When do you start on leaves?

October through December, once they actually start dropping. Book early if you want a spot in the run.
